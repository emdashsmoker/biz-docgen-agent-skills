- Fill the basic report information at step 0. Fill the other sections at step 1
- The writing method is in the references/report.md Diagnosis Design section. The order of sections from Procurement Background to Utilization Design is the step order of the problem-solving methodology
- Write entries in Korean. Design language (situation, problem, key question, diagnostic question numbers, hypothesis numbers) goes only in this file and 02_스토리라인.md

## Procurement Background

- (Stated purpose) Enter the RFP's background and purpose sentences verbatim [확인]
- (Procurement circumstances) Why the client procures at this moment, and supporting materials. Revision cycle of a higher-level plan, audit findings, first year of a new system, reorganization, budget review, shortcomings of a previous contract, poor performance, entry into a new business, investment attraction, and so on [확인]

## Problem Statement

- (Situation) The current state of the client and the target program. Enter only confirmed facts [확인]
- (Problem) The gap between the state the client aims to reach and the situation. Give the supporting material for the target state alongside [확인]
- (Key question) One question the whole report answers. A question about the solution direction tied to the client's decision [확인]
- (Scope and constraints) The areas the solution covers, excluded areas, and constraints (budget, laws, schedule, and so on) [확인]

## Diagnostic Questions

- An issue tree that asks why the problem arose. The root question is "why did the problem in the problem statement arise"
- Every question asks for causes. A sub-question splits the cause candidates of its parent question
- Put the draft answer to the key question in the judgment statement of the direction (draft) section in the table of contents design table of 02_스토리라인.md
- (Decomposition criterion) One of time, structure, or type. Questions under the same parent are divided by the same criterion. Questions under the same parent do not overlap. Together they cover the whole parent question
- (Priority) One of 상 (high), 중 (medium), 하 (low). The criteria are the size of the effect on the problem and how far the client can change it

## Hypotheses

- At least one initial hypothesis per leaf diagnostic question. The materials for initial hypotheses are the RFP, the client's public materials, and material the user submitted
- The answer to a parent question is set in the synthesis step by bundling the verdicts below it
- (Support condition) What the data looks like when it supports the hypothesis
- (Refutation condition) What the data looks like when it rejects the hypothesis. Rewrite a hypothesis that no result could reject
- (Data type) 문헌 (literature) or 현장 (field). Field applies to hypotheses that can be finalized only with questionnaires, interviews, internal data, and so on

## Utilization Design

- Writing principles that make the final results usable in the client's decisions. This table stays only in the diagnosis file
    + (Utilization obstacles) Points where the final results cannot serve the decision. For example the absence of the quantitative grounds the persuasion targets require, initiatives missing their responsible department, alternatives disconnected from field demand, and so on
    + (Final result requirements) The conditions the final results must meet to overcome the obstacle, with a one-line test
    + (Design principles) Writing principles that secure the final result requirements. For example stating the responsible department and required budget for each initiative, presenting comparison tables of alternatives on the same criteria, and so on
    + (Reflecting sections) The sections where the design principle applies. Enter after the table of contents is finalized at step 2
